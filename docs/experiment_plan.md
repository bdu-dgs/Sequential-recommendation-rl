# Experiment plan

> **What actually ran (2026-10-26):** all of it locally with `scripts/run_local.py`. Steps 5, 6, 8 in full; step 7
> only the narrow-coverage regime (5 seeds) plus seed-0 κ/λ diagnostics; plus a mechanism stage (`diag`, 3 seeds).
> Not run: broad coverage, environment mechanisms, α sweep, inheritance ablations. Results: `reports/research_report.md`.

Job lists are generated by `scripts/make_jobs.py` and run with `scripts/run_local.py` (or on RIS with
`ris/submit_all.sh`, see `ris/README.md`).
All policy numbers are simulated returns in KuaiRecFO-v1. Main results use seeds 0–4; mechanism ablations use
seeds 0–2.

## Step 3 — real-data supervised evidence (done locally)
`scripts/train_response_model.py`: GRU two-tower model vs global-mean, item-mean, and user+item-bias baselines.
- Temporal test (Sep 1–5): seen, FO, and unseen users.
- Full ranking over the fully-observed matrix: NDCG@10/50, Spearman, top-10 true watch ratio, AUC, ECE.
- Selected model: `response_model_wd`, seeds 0–2.

## Step 4 — simulator and data (done locally)
`scripts/characterize_env.py`: reference policies (random, popularity, logging policy, oracle greedy) and
dataset coverage statistics. Defaults: logging T = 0.1, ε = 0.05, 200k transitions.

## Step 5 — offline baselines (RIS stage s5b)
random, log popularity, behavior (the logging policy), reward-regression ranker, BC, offline Double DQN,
discrete CQL (α = 1), Cal-QL, and discrete BCQ (τ = 0.3). Each learned method trains for 30k gradient steps,
batch 256. Evaluation: val every 5k steps; at the end, one greedy episode per test user.

## Step 6 — offline→online (RIS stage s6)
Budget: 50k environment transitions on env-train users, 16 parallel envs, 4 gradient steps per 16
transitions, ε-greedy 0.1→0.02. Validation evaluation every 2.5k transitions, including at 0. Methods:
- from scratch: Double DQN, RLPD-style;
- bandit: NeuralLinear-UCB;
- fine-tune from CQL: naive Double DQN, constant CQL, decayed CQL, Cal-QL;
- support-weighted CQL with oracle, BC, uniform (strength matched at the start), uniform-tracked (strength
  re-matched to the oracle at every update), shuffled (fixed item permutation), or inverted support.

Inheritance defaults: copy weights and target net, reset Adam, keep 50% offline data in each batch.

**Primary metrics:**
- worst drop below the starting validation return;
- area under the validation return curve;
- interactions needed to exceed the logging policy;
- final test return.

## Step 7 — ablations (RIS stages s7a–c)
1. **Data coverage:** narrow (ε = 0, head of 500 items: zero support elsewhere) and broad (ε = 0.2, T = 0.3).
2. **Environment mechanism** (3 seeds): fatigue β ∈ {0, 0.6}, patience ∈ {2, 5}, observation noise σ = 0.3.
3. **Conservatism strength:** fine-tuning α ∈ {0.3, 3} for constant CQL, oracle-support, and matched-uniform.
4. **Support temperature** κ ∈ {0.3, 3}; no online counts (λ = 0) for oracle, uniform, and shuffled.
5. **Inheritance:** keep Adam, reset target net, offline ratio ∈ {0, 0.25}, applied to naive and oracle-support
   fine-tuning.

## Step 8 — simulator exploitation, generalization, OPE (RIS stages s8a–b)
- **Exploitation:** interact with the *learned* simulator (response-model predictions) and evaluate in the FO
  simulator. Report the gap between the two.
- **Generalization** (test users): preference drift δ ∈ {0.2, 0.5}, cold start (0 or 2 warm-start items),
  noise 0.3, fatigue 0.6.
- **OPE** on simulated logs with known propensities: one-step IPS, SNIPS, DM, DR vs exact ground truth, with
  ESS, max weight share, and unsupported mass.

## Analysis (step 9, local after results return)
- Mean ± 95% bootstrap CI over seeds.
- Validation curves of fine-tuning, with the logging-policy level as a reference.
- Drop vs coverage regime; support of the chosen actions; coverage and long tail.
- OPE error vs unsupported mass; failure trajectories.

**Decision rule for the hypothesis:** support weighting "helps" only if oracle-support fine-tuning beats both
the uniform-tracked (strength-matched over time) and the shuffled-support controls, on the drop or on the area under the curve, with
non-overlapping 95% CIs in the base setting. Otherwise the result is reported as negative.
